02 · Custom meal prep

Your rules,
her hands.

Most kitchens sell a box. Amy keeps a list. No tomatoes in the salad. Jerk sauce in a cup. A milder pot for the children and the usual one for everyone else. Tell her how many people, which nights, and what the house will not eat. She answers with a menu and a number.

Pickup in 78756, or a drop on the supper route. Farther than that is part of the quote, not a promise on this page.
